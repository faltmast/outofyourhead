# outofyourhead.org

Website of Ro Quetzalcoatl. Plain HTML, no build step. Live at https://outofyourhead.org.
Two people work on it: Ro and Alexis. Every push to `main` goes live automatically (Vercel), about 30 seconds later.

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole homepage (text, styles, dragon animation)
- `impressum.html`, `datenschutz.html`: legal pages (German law, keep them)
- `img/`: photos and the flute video. Keep images under 400 KB.
- `fonts/`: Young Serif (titles) and DM Sans (text). No other fonts.

## How to work
1. Always `git pull` first. The other person may have changed something.
2. Make the change. Keep the design: colours and fonts from Ro's brand (ro-brand skill).
3. Open `index.html` in the browser and check it, also at phone width.
4. Commit with a short message that says what changed, e.g. "Add 13.12. journey".
5. `git push`. Live in about 30 seconds.

If Ro asks for a change: explain it to him in Spanish, show him what you changed, and ask before pushing.
Never push the `.vercel/` folder.

## Text rules
Same as ro-brand: no healing, therapy, transformation, enlightenment. No dashes as punctuation. One action per section.
